About Us

Connecting trusted brands with growing markets across the UAE and worldwide.

Established in the United Arab Emirates, UP Nutrition FZC is a dynamic trading company focused on the wholesale, distribution, import, and export of trusted consumer products. We work with established suppliers and recognized brands to connect quality products with businesses across local and international markets. Our understanding of market demand allows us to identify high-potential products and provide practical supply solutions for our partners.

Our Mission

To supply quality FMCG, food, health, and beauty products through dependable sourcing, competitive pricing, and efficient distribution. We aim to support our customers’ growth by providing the right products, responsive service, and reliable wholesale and export solutions.

Our Vision

To become a trusted regional trading and distribution partner, connecting reputable brands and quality consumer products with growing markets across the UAE and internationally. We aspire to be recognized for our reliability, market knowledge, and operational excellence.
